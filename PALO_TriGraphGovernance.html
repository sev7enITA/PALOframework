THE GOVERNANCE NEED

Correct facts can still produce an invalid action.

01

Stale or incomplete context

A relevant restriction is omitted, an approval expires or the resource changes after review.

Control: mandatory dependency closure, explicit exclusions, validity intervals and external version bindings.

02

Authority inferred from text

A plausible answer, a high confidence score or an external instruction is treated as permission.

Control: separate evidence quality from authority. Evaluate curated guards and an exact mandate scope.

03

Uncertain effects and unsafe retries

A timeout hides a completed write. Retrying can duplicate the effect; compensation may be impossible.

Control: reconciliation before another write, a new mandate for recovery and explicit limits on reversibility.

TRANSPARENCY · EXPLAINABILITY · ACCOUNTABILITY
Trace the rule and preserve the right to challenge.
Transparency
Expose the data origin, selected and excluded inputs, release versions and capability boundary.
Explainability
Provide deterministic rule results and replay inputs. A receipt explains the policy evaluation; it does not expose hidden model reasoning or prove the real-world effect.
Accountability
Assign owners to curation, admission, execution and review. Preserve original decisions, record challenges and issue linked corrections.
Correction workflow
Assertion corrected→Reverse dependencies→Contexts to reassess→Accountable review
Production alerting, service-level targets, persistence and independent review remain implementation requirements.
