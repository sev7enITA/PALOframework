How to read these results

Configurations, not interchangeable models. Provider, reasoning, prompt, adapter, output ceiling and revision are part of the observation. A catalogue context window is not the amount of document text processed. High reasoning does not represent equal compute across providers.

Known, unknown and missing remain distinct. No response is not a semantic error. A charge without evidence is not zero. Primary labels remain frozen, even when an internal challenge makes a label questionable. The sensitivity lens is explicitly post-hoc.

Qualification remains open. There is no independent custodian, independently adjudicated domain gold or validated measure here. Repeated calls within one authored family do not establish general performance. No overall score, confidence interval or quality rank is assigned.

Interface references: Arena’s category navigation and Artificial Analysis trends. PALO reports separate internal procurement metrics and does not claim equivalent validation.
