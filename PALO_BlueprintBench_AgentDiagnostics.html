Study design

Interpretation and limitations

Results belong to the reported model, prompt, runtime and hardware configuration.

01

Development material

Cases are selected for internal development and may have been exposed to the project. Small samples and repeated calls do not establish general performance.

02

Advisory assessment

These tests exercise the agent prompt and output validator via a local model endpoint, not the full agent application workflow. Labels and explanations are model-generated suggestions; matching quotations do not verify their conclusions.

03

Independent review

Human expertise, independent adjudication, external custody and measurement validation remain separate requirements.
