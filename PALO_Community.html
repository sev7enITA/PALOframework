Open roles

Where specialist review strengthens PALO

Contribution routes are organized around concrete review responsibilities. Role titles describe collaboration needs, not employment offers or formal appointments.

Open Source Community Lead

Coordinate issue triage, contributor onboarding, review cadence and inclusive participation practices.

Ethics and Policy Advisor

Challenge policy mappings, rights impacts and claims that could overstate legal or ethical authority.

Technical Documentation Lead

Improve implementation guidance, traceability and the clarity of evidence and boundary statements.

Data Privacy Specialist

Review data handling, minimization, retention and privacy risk across governance workflows.

AI and ML Research Advisor

Test assumptions, research methods and operational claims against current technical evidence.

Peer Review Committee

Conduct structured, conflict-aware review of major releases, methods and public evidence claims.
