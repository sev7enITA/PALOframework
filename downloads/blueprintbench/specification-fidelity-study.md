# Specification Fidelity Study

Status: RFC, 11 November 2026. Proposed first validation study for the PALO Blueprint evaluation programme. No recruitment, custodian appointment, scored cohort or independent qualification is established by this document.

## Research question

When procurement documents are translated into a development specification, which obligations, permissions, prohibitions, owners, conditions and exceptions survive, and how much human work is needed to repair the result?

The subject is a frozen system configuration, including the model, provider/runtime, adapter, prompt and wire contract. The interpretation track receives the same human-checked extracted source units. Application ingestion and full-repository exports remain separate engineering tests. PALO remains free; private evaluation services are a discovery hypothesis, not a new product price or commitment.

## First cohort

Start with procurement. An initial target of 150–200 adjudicated propositions is a scoping estimate, not a statistical sufficiency claim. Select independent source families and document their rights, provenance, difficulty and exposure. Determine family count and uncertainty design after a calibration study. Three repeats estimate within-configuration variation; they do not create three independent document families.

Use two blinded domain annotators and adjudication before inspecting evaluated outputs. Record allowed alternatives, forbidden inferences, answerability and unresolved ambiguity. Predeclare critical source rules and the policy for new unsupported critical assertions. Include current frontier baselines and diverse challengers, chosen and configured before the sealed cohort. Do not infer unsuitability from a rejected provider parameter. No inference spending beyond the existing authorisation is implied.

## Measures

- **Critical source-rule inversion rate:** distinct required, assessable source rules materially inverted in the output, divided by the fixed number of such source rules in the assigned scope, multiplied by 100. Count each source rule once. An output that contradicts itself still contains the inversion. Report numerator, denominator and per-family outcomes.
- **Unsupported critical assertions:** a separate count of unique unsupported critical propositions, including assertions without a legitimate source-rule match. Do not hide them because the first metric has a source-rule denominator.
- **Coverage:** recovered required source propositions / all assigned required source propositions. Empty output has zero coverage, even when it has zero inversions. Report abstentions, unresolved cases and omissions separately.
- **Human correction effort:** active minutes and edit actions required to reach a prespecified accepted blueprint, plus abandoned sessions and acceptance rate. Report time spent on unsuccessful tasks; do not present successful-session timing as the whole workload. Missing observations remain unknown.

Use a non-compensatory accepted-outcome contract: zero defined critical violations, required coverage/quality thresholds and evidence gates, fixed before the evaluation. Preserve reported transport/completion separately. No composite PBI formula or thresholds are adopted here.

## Validate the measurement

Construct a separately labelled calibration set containing source-verified semantic mutations: may/must, negation, owner, threshold, exception and condition changes. Include unchanged and valid-alternative controls. Keep mutation labels hidden from annotators. Estimate detection, false alarms and disagreement by defect type and source family. Do not use the same mutations to tune and then independently validate the scorer. Publicly release rights-cleared calibration or retired material; retain live evaluation keys under agreed external custody.

Uncertainty must respect family clustering and paired configurations. Claim-level Wilson intervals can be illustrations but are not credible independent-sample intervals for a few related passages. Few families cannot be repaired by bootstrap alone. Determine whether results support a ranking before publishing any ordered inference.

## Qualification decision

A local hash proves byte identity, not trustworthy timing, authorship, truth or independence. Before a sealed run, an appointed external custodian must acknowledge the exact manifest digest and receipt time through an independently verifiable record. A signature on a freely chosen local timestamp alone is insufficient. Publish signer identity, role, conflicts, verification method and custody scope without releasing the active gold.

A proposed qualification record binds the exact run/configuration, corpus and gold commitments, protocol and evaluator versions, evidence manifest, reproduction scope, unresolved limitations, decision, decision date and appeal route. It requires an identified domain adjudication authority and an independent reproduction/qualification reviewer with declared conflicts and verifiable signatures. A custodian's receipt alone does not qualify semantic quality.

The maintainer may publish the signed decision, but may not supply missing independent approval for their own submission. Until roles are appointed and the record is reviewed, the state remains pending/unqualified. Qualification applies only to the named configuration, cohort and protocol, never to a model universally. The current evaluator continues to emit qualified=false; this RFC does not override it.

## Annotation reuse

Maintain a versioned proposition bank keyed by task, source digest, precise scope, gold/rubric version and adjudicated semantic identity, retaining modality, negation, actor, time and exceptions. Similar text or embeddings can suggest a match but cannot establish equivalence. Human review is required for new or context-changed classes and for the raw-output-to-atomic-proposition mapping. Reuse reference judgments only within the approved equivalence scope; attach their provenance to each output occurrence and audit a blinded sample. A new source, scope or gold version invalidates unreviewed reuse. Do not expose active keys to systems or submitters.

## Next acceptance gates

1. Resolve and regression-test critical-count consistency, outcome semantics and raw-to-claim provenance; publish a single executable contract for the scoped evaluator.
2. Establish external commitment verification and named independent decision roles.
3. Release the rights-cleared harness, prompts, schemas, adapters and raw diagnostic outputs, or state which evidence cannot be redistributed and why. Aggregates alone are not independent reproducibility.
4. Run and publish the mutation calibration study and family-aware uncertainty analysis.
5. Conduct business-user sessions and five discovery conversations with procurement, audit and systems-integration stakeholders before committing to a private-services offering. Invitations require separate authorisation.

A dedicated domain can provide a distinct public home; institutional credibility depends on these verifiable responsibilities and evidence.
