## Internal testing badge

The badge reads **Internal testing** and **Evidence linked**. It identifies a dated research test record. It does not assert success, model quality, certification, independent review, a production approval or a PALO Blueprint Index.

Use the complete badge without removing the status. Link it to the exact model/configuration record and display the campaign date, task scope and completion status nearby. A blocked configuration must say “Pre-call blocked” and must not display a badge implying an executed test. Failed tests retain their failed status. The badge alone is not a tamper-proof credential.
