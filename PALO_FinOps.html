Allocation rules
Rules match dimensions in priority order. Weights, versions and effective dates are explicit. Missing weights leave costs unallocated. Preview does not change source amounts.

04 / Freeze a reproducible metric
Period snapshot
Includes every selected workload and all its attempted tasks in the UTC window. Cost and outcome copies remain in the exported snapshot. Incomplete, estimated or mismatched data produces an unavailable unit cost.
