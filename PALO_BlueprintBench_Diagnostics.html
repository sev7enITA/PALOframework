Review design and limitations

The sample contains 18 verification probes, selected as two per original draft-label class in each case, plus 18 source-bound draft translations. It is deliberately targeted and cannot represent the full cohort.

Author model identities and prior labels were hidden in reviewer prompts. The reviewer families overlap the tested cohort. Reviewers can share errors, and model agreement is not independent human adjudication.

GLM, Gemini and the local Qwen model provide additional internal evidence. Missing or incomplete reviews stay visible. No PALO Blueprint Index or revised ranking is assigned from this exercise.

Delivered checks and further development
  • Versioned source-bound proposition bank and explicit draft rubric. Independent expert gold remains pending.
  • Replayable review-to-export harness, strict identifiers and fixed denominators. No new Workbench document-ingestion or 100 MB test is claimed here.
  • Structural failures and semantic outcomes are separate. A schema-valid judgment can still be wrong.
  • Exposed sources remain ineligible for independent qualification. Local hashes establish byte identity, not external custody.
  • Next study: subtle alterations, instruction-bearing candidates, order-shuffled repetitions, unanswerable items, new independent source families and blind human adjudication.
